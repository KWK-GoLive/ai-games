💬 Part 1 · Chatbots

  1. 1 Warm-up · 5 min · no score
    Guess the Chatbot
    Predict what a real chatbot wrote. Why is it never quite the same twice?
  2. 2 Learn · 25–30 min
    Be the LLM
    Guess the next word, train a tiny model, and see how a chatbot answers questions, and why it can sound sure and still be wrong.
  3. 3 Challenge · about 20 min · scoreboard
    LLM Arena
    Be the model against the clock: count, write, roll the temperature dice and answer chats exactly as the model would.

🤖 Part 2 · AI agents

  1. 4 Warm-up · 6 min · no score
    Watch a Real Agent
    Replay a real AI agent turning a file into Excel and Word files, then check its work.
  2. 5 Learn · 25–30 min
    Be the Agent
    The desk (context window), reading files, tools, the agent loop, hidden instructions, and who's in charge.
  3. 6 Challenge · about 20 min · scoreboard
    Agent Arena
    Run the agent: pack the desk, search, pick tools, ask the data questions, catch planted orders and deliver a real Excel file. No typing.
