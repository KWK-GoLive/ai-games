Checking AI data work

Use this whenever a chatbot or AI agent works with your data: a spreadsheet, a report, a summary of numbers. Every check comes from something you saw happen in the games.

  1. 🧮
    Did a tool or program compute the numbers?

    A language model writes numbers as likely-looking words. Trust a number more when you can see it came from a calculator, a formula or code.

    You saw it: Be the LLM, Level 7 (17 + 5) · Be the Agent, Level 3
  2. ✅
    Recompute one total yourself.

    Pick one important number and check it in the real file, for example with a SUM in Excel. A wrong total can look completely normal.

    You saw it: Be the Agent, Level 4 (the memo total)
  3. 🕳️
    How were missing or odd rows handled?

    Ask: were any rows empty, strange or left out? Did the totals include them? Read what the AI says about them against the real row.

    You saw it: Watch a Real Agent (the July 5 row)
  4. 🔍
    Does any claim go beyond the columns you have?

    Profit needs costs. Causes need more than a table of sales. Trends need enough time. If the data can't show it, the sentence shouldn't say it.

    You saw it: Watch a Real Agent ("margin" with no costs)
  5. 🔒
    Did personal or confidential data stay in approved tools?

    What you paste or upload is sent to the company running the chatbot. Use the tools your organisation approves, and share only what the task needs.

    You saw it: Be the Agent, Level 6
  6. 📝
    Ask it to show its formula, code or source.

    "Show me the formula you used" or "quote the sentence from the file". Then check that formula or quote yourself.

    You saw it: Be the Agent, Levels 2 and 3
